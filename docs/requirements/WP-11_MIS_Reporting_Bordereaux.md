# WP-11 — MIS, Reporting & Bordereaux
**Version:** 1.0 (for business review) | **Date:** 12 July 2026
**Depends on:** WP-07 | **Feeds:** WP-12
**Rule ID prefix:** MIS-xxx

---

## 1. Reporting Principles
- **MIS-001.** Every report is tenant-scoped by default (TEN-001); only platform roles see cross-tenant views.
- **MIS-002.** All money figures reconcile to the three-way match (PAY-030); a report that cannot tie to the match is a defect, not a feature.

## 2. Report Catalogue (v1)

**Platform (licensed entity)**
- Daily sales register: applications, quotes, conversions, STP rate, referral rate, declines by category — by regime, channel, tenant.
- Exceptions dashboard: paid-not-issued, issued-not-registered (PAY-030), SLA breaches (REF-012), registration-failure queue age.
- Commission receivable ledger: due from insurer per policy, 10-business-day aging (REG-004), received/outstanding.
- Payout payable ledger: accrued/paid/clawed per downstream partner (QR-021/022).
- Compliance pack: screening volumes, match dispositions, EDD cases, STR referrals count (no case detail), training attestation status (KYC-032).
- Conduct monitor: per-tenant referral/decline/cancellation/mismatch rates vs thresholds (TEN-011).

**Insurer/TPA**
- **Bordereau (the contractual core):** periodic statement — daily file + monthly signed statement **[INSURER]** — listing per policy: policy no., insured (per agreed PII scope), plan, premium, VAT, issue date, registration status/date, endorsements, cancellations, refund amounts, commission computed. Format: agreed CSV/Excel layout per partner annex; delivery via SFTP/API **[INSURER]**.
- Referral queue performance: volumes, decision mix, SLA compliance.
- Registration-failure log (their side of PAY-023).

**Broker / typing centre / affiliate**
- Own production report: applications, issued policies, payout accruals and statements; affiliate sees clicks/conversions/payouts only.

## 3. Regulatory & Audit Hooks
- **MIS-010.** Data extracts for CBUAE/DHA/DoH inspection requests: full application-to-policy audit trail retrievable by policy number, EID, or date range within 1 business day (retention per KYC-030).
- **MIS-011.** PDPL data-subject access request (DSAR) extract: all personal data held for a given EID, generated by compliance role only.
- **MIS-012.** Monthly management pack for business owner: GWP, policies, commission income, payout cost, net margin, by regime/channel; cancellation and refund trends; funnel conversion by journey step (identifies where blue-collar users drop).

## 4. Data Definitions
- **MIS-020.** Single metrics dictionary maintained as an appendix (definitions of "application", "conversion", "issued", "active", "STP rate", "referral rate", "GWP") — every report must use these definitions; the dictionary is sign-off content in WP-12 so business and tech never argue about numbers later.
